HOW TO LOAD
The Semrush MCP surface is read-only for Position Tracking, so this has to be
pasted into the UI: Position Tracking > Settings > Keywords > add manually.
Paste one tier at a time and tag the batch with the tier letter before moving
on, so the visibility chart segments. Load in tier order and stop at a tier
boundary if the keyword allowance runs out.

TWO SETTINGS TO FIX WHILE YOU ARE IN THERE
1. Location. The campaign reports "clinical simulation" at volume 30 against a
   national 140, and requesting use_volume=national through the API does not
   change it, so the campaign is scoped to something narrower than United
   States. Set location to United States, device desktop.
2. Remove "ai simulation". Zero national volume and the SERP is consumer AI
   video tooling, not clinical simulation.
