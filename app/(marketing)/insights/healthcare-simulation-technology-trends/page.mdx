## What matters in 2026

Medical simulation technology now includes voice-based AI patients alongside established standardized patient, mannequin, and screen-based methods. The useful question is which practice or assessment job each method can do.

## Key Insights

- Standardized patients remain important for high-stakes assessment where human judgment and physical presence matter.
- AI patients can add repeatable voice-based practice between scheduled encounters.
- Feedback should name the framework used and cite evidence from the learner's transcript.
- Programs should evaluate learner performance directly and avoid claiming patient outcomes that have not been studied.

## The Research

The evidence base for AI patient simulation is still developing, so product claims should stay narrow. ClinicalSim provides physician-authored cases, framework-based scoring, and transcript-grounded feedback. It does not claim that a practice score predicts a patient survey, malpractice claim, or clinical outcome.
